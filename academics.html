Academics

A complete educational journey from Kindergarten through Senior Secondary School.

Our Structure

Four Levels of Education

Ruesab Private School provides structured, high-quality learning at every stage of a child’s development.

Kindergarten (KG)

A warm, stimulating early-childhood programme that builds foundational literacy, numeracy, social skills and a love of learning through play and guided discovery.

Primary School

A solid primary curriculum that develops strong reading, writing, mathematics and science skills, alongside character formation and creative expression.

Junior Secondary School (JSS)

A broad junior secondary programme covering core subjects, practical skills and character education, preparing students for senior secondary pathways.

Senior Secondary School (SSS)

Specialised Science, Arts and Commercial pathways leading to national examinations (WAEC/NECO) and readiness for tertiary education and careers.
