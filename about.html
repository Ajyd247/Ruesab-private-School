About Ruesab

A community dedicated to academic excellence, character formation and preparing young people for a purposeful future.

Who We Are

An Institution Serving Every Stage of Learning

Ruesab Private School was founded with a clear purpose: to provide high-quality education from Kindergarten through Senior Secondary School, developing the mind, character and leadership capacity of every student.

We believe that true education balances rigorous academics with strong values, practical skills and a supportive community. Our dual-campus structure allows us to serve a wider community while maintaining one standard of excellence.

From Kindergarten and Primary through Junior and Senior Secondary, students experience a curriculum that prepares them for the next stage of learning, national examinations, tertiary education and responsible citizenship.

Our Structure

Educational Levels

Ruesab Private School serves students across four continuous stages of learning.

Kindergarten

Early childhood foundation in a caring, stimulating environment.

Primary

Strong literacy, numeracy and character development.

Junior Secondary

Broad curriculum preparing students for senior pathways.

Senior Secondary

Specialised pathways towards examinations and tertiary education.

Our Journey

School History

2006

Founding of Ruesab

Ruesab Private School opened its doors with a small cohort of students and a clear vision for excellence.

2008

First Full Senior Secondary Cohort

The school celebrated its first set of graduates sitting for national examinations with outstanding results.

2010

Expansion of Facilities

New science laboratories, ICT suite and sports facilities were commissioned to support modern learning.

2010

Totowu Branch Opens

A second campus was established to serve growing demand while maintaining the same high standards.

2024 – Present

Continued Growth & Innovation

Ongoing investment in teacher development, digital learning and student support programmes.
