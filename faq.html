Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Ruesab Private School.

Visit our Admission page, download and fill the application form and submit the required documents. You will then be invited for screening and assessment.
Applicants should be age-appropriate for the intended class, have completed primary education (for JSS 1) or relevant secondary records, perform satisfactorily in the entrance assessment, and be willing to uphold the school’s values and code of conduct. Full details are on the Admission page.
We offer Kindergarten, Primary (1–6), Junior Secondary (JSS 1–3) and Senior Secondary (SSS 1–3). At the senior level, students can choose Science, Arts or Commercial pathways in preparation for national examinations and tertiary education.
Ruesab has two campuses. The Headquarters is at 211 Isuti Road, Akasear House, Egan/Igando, P.O Box 393, Ikotun, Lagos. Totowu Branch is at 42 Learning Lane, 1st Caterpillar Area, Ilugbo Totowu town, Ogun State. Both operate under the same school brand and administration.
Yes. In addition to the Headquarters, we have a Branch Campus at Totowu. Both campuses follow the same curriculum, values and standards. Placement depends on age, class level and available spaces.
Typically: completed application form, birth certificate (or age declaration), previous school report cards/transcripts, recent passport photographs, transfer certificate (for transfers), and a medical fitness report upon offer. Check the Admission page for the current list.
Fees cover tuition, basic learning resources and selected activities. A detailed fee schedule is provided with the offer letter. Limited scholarships and payment plans may be available – please contact the admissions office for current information.
Transport options may be available depending on routes and demand. Please contact the school office for current arrangements and fees related to school transport.
We offer sports (football, athletics, basketball and more), debate, STEM clubs, music, cultural activities, leadership programmes and other clubs. Activities help students discover talents, build confidence and develop teamwork.
