How to Apply

Application Process

A clear five-step journey from application to enrolment.

1

Submit Application

Complete the application form and upload required documents online or at the school office.

2

Screening / Interview

Shortlisted candidates and parents are invited for a brief interaction with the admissions team.

3

Assessment

Students sit a placement / entrance assessment appropriate to the intended class level.

4

Admission Decision

Successful applicants receive a formal offer letter with details of fees and next steps.

5

Enrolment

Accept the offer, complete payment and registration, and collect the joining pack.

Requirements

Admission Requirements

  • ✓ Age-appropriate for the intended class
  • ✓ Completed primary education (for JSS 1) or previous secondary records (for higher classes)
  • ✓ Satisfactory performance in entrance assessment
  • ✓ Positive recommendation from previous school (where applicable)
  • ✓ Willingness to uphold school values and code of conduct
Documents

Required Documents

  • ✓ Completed application form
  • ✓ Birth certificate or age declaration
  • ✓ Previous school report cards / transcripts
  • ✓ Two (2) Passport photographs (recent)
  • ✓ Transfer certificate (for transfer students)
