About Ruesab

A Complete Educational Journey

Ruesab Private School is a co-educational institution offering Kindergarten, Primary, Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary education under one standard of excellence, discipline and care.

Our dual-campus model allows us to serve families across the region while maintaining one unified curriculum, values and administrative leadership.

What Sets Ruesab Apart

Academic Excellence

Consistently strong examination results and a curriculum that prepares students for tertiary education and life beyond school.

Qualified Teachers

Dedicated, well-trained educators who inspire curiosity, critical thinking and a genuine love of learning.

Modern Environment

Well-equipped classrooms, science laboratories, ICT suites and sports facilities that support 21st-century learning.

Character & Discipline

Our School

Four Levels of Education

From the earliest years through Senior Secondary — one standard of excellence.

Kindergarten

A nurturing early-childhood programme that builds foundations in literacy, numeracy, social skills and a love of learning.

Primary School

Strong primary curriculum in reading, writing, mathematics and science, with character formation and creativity.

Junior Secondary

Broad JSS programme covering core subjects, practical skills and values that prepare students for senior pathways.

Senior Secondary

Science, Arts and Commercial pathways leading to national examinations and tertiary readiness.
